# Flo Hack — Hackathon Brief (Reference)

> **This document is the fixed reference.** It records *what the hackathon asks of us and how we are judged* — nothing about our product. It does not change as our plan evolves.
> **Our implementation lives separately** in [README.md](./README.md) (the built app — source of truth), [PROPOSAL.md](./PROPOSAL.md) (product plan), and [AGENT-ARCHITECTURE.md](./AGENT-ARCHITECTURE.md) (the agent).
> **Why separate:** if our product later diverges or grows beyond the hackathon, this brief stays clean so we can always check our work against the original ask, and show judges a clear line from requirement → implementation.

---

## 1. Session

**Flo Hack: Build AI Use Cases for Smarter Ways of Working.**

**Central challenge:** How can we use AI to materially improve the way our teams work, collaborate, manage delivery, practice Agile, and create business value?

## 2. Philosophy

Solve meaningful, real-world problems rather than demonstrating AI technology. Every solution must show a clear connection between the **problem → the AI intervention → a measurable improvement**.

```
problem  ->  AI intervention  ->  measurable improvement
```

### Mindset shift the organisers ask for

| From | To |
|---|---|
| "What can we build with AI?" | "What meaningful problem can we solve better with AI?" |
| Technology-first thinking | Problem- and outcome-first thinking |
| AI demonstration | Working solution to a real organizational challenge |
| Interesting prototype | Prototype with a path to adoption and measurable value |

## 3. Judging criteria (what we are scored on)

We are **not** judged on the most sophisticated AI. We are judged on:

| # | Criterion | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Meaningful problem | A real problem that affects users, teams, delivery or business value |
| 02 | AI intervention | A clear explanation of how AI improves the workflow, decision or outcome |
| 03 | Working concept | A prototype or demo that proves the core idea can work |
| 04 | Adoption path | A credible route to implementation, controls and measurable impact |

## 4. Challenge areas (choose one primary)

1. **Ways of Working** — remove friction from everyday collaboration and routine admin
2. **Project & Delivery Management** — make delivery predictable, visible and fact-based
3. **Agile Practices** — make agile events and artefacts outcome-focused
4. **Team Culture & Engagement** — help teams stay connected, learning and sustainable
5. **Metrics & Value Measurement** — turn outcome and flow metrics into actionable insight
6. **Open Innovation** — any AI idea that makes the organization work better

**Our chosen primary:** Agile Practices (with benefits spilling into the other five). *This choice is ours; it is recorded here because it anchors how we map to the brief — see the traceability table in PROPOSAL.md §1.*

---

## 5. How to use this document

- Treat sections 1–4 as **immutable** during the hackathon — they are the organisers' ask.
- When we add a product capability, check it against §2 (does it solve a real problem with a measurable improvement?) and §3 (which criterion does it serve?).
- If a feature serves our product vision but **not** the brief, that is fine — but it belongs in the "Vision / beyond hackathon" column of the traceability table, not in the demo scope.
