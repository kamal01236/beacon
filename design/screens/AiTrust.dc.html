Guardrails

In the product
Preview before every write. Nothing reaches Jira or Azure DevOps without a before→after diff and a confirm.
Labelled at the destination. Every pushed comment carries “AI-assisted · accepted by <name>”.
Jira stays the system of record. Beacon writes to few fields and owns none of them.
No individual performance scoring. Manager views are team-level by design, not by configuration.
